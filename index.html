About Us

A modern take on pre-owned, built right here in Seattle.

Argaw Auto is an independent vehicle sourcing business founded on a simple idea: that buying a used car should feel as straightforward as buying anything else. We hand-pick every vehicle we sell, verify the full history through Carfax, and inspect each car end-to-end before it ever reaches a customer.

Whether you are looking for a daily driver, a weekend coupe, or your first luxury car, we are here to help you find it. We believe in transparent pricing, honest conversations, and treating every customer the way we would want to be treated. Questions? Reach out anytime at aargaw@uw.edu.
